Trust · Capital · Investment

What the club is about

A club of people who know each other, decide together, and keep every member’s account open to that member.

Built on trust

Members join through people they know, and the club’s decisions are made by its members under its own rules.

Clear records

Every contribution is recorded with a numbered receipt, and each member can see their own account and statements online.

Run by members

Officers do the day-to-day work, important decisions need a second person to approve them, and the board oversees the whole.

PROPOSED ROADMAP

Explore the future of member rewards

We are exploring MC Points and a possible Millionaires Club token. MCC is the working ticker, with MCT under consideration. The design and timing are still being evaluated.

Member benefits

Define useful benefits and the activities that could earn recognition, with clear limits and a member support process.

MC Points pilot

Proposed next step. Test earning and redeeming points with a small member group and measure whether the benefits are useful.

Token feasibility

Evaluate wallets, issuance rules, operating costs and the requirements for a potential token after the pilot.

Token availability, supply, conversion rules and any launch date remain undecided. For questions about the proposal, contact the club.
